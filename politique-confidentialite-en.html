Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Data collected

Through the contact form, we only collect the data you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone / WhatsApp number (optional)

Purpose of processing

This data is used exclusively to answer your support request, prepare your free consultation and follow up with you about your study project. It is never sold or transferred to third parties.

Data retention

Your data is kept for the time needed to process your request, then archived for a maximum period of 3 years after our last exchange, unless otherwise required by law.

Your rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, write to us at contact@einsteinservicesvoyages.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

Cookies

This website uses no advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Only technical cookies may be set for the website to function properly.
