Why choose us

Bring your study project to life

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions our students ask most often.

How do I choose my study destination?

We analyse your profile and your project to guide you towards a suitable programme and destination.

Which documents are needed for an application file?

The required documents depend on the service and the procedure. The list must be confirmed by the agency.

How long does it take to obtain a visa?

Timelines depend on the destination and the procedure. Applicable timelines are to be confirmed with the agency.

Do you help with finding accommodation?

Student housing via Nexroom and the certificate of accommodation for the visa are mentioned in our services. Details are to be confirmed with the agency.

Are your services paid?

Fees and payment terms are to be confirmed with the agency.

Can students work while studying abroad?

The rules depend on the country and the student's status. Applicable information is to be confirmed with the agency.

What is the AVI?

The AVI is one of the financing solutions mentioned in our services. Its procedure and conditions are to be confirmed with the agency.
